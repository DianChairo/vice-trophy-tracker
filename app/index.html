Roadmap to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the set 4 out of 10 in difficulty and about 200 hours, in a single playthrough, with no online trophies and no DLC needed. The story takes about 60 to 80 hours across five chapters. Keep cheats off for the whole run — using any cheat disables trophies and saving (PowerPyx via GTABase).

Missable trophies

PowerPyx flags exactly one trophy as possibly missable. Every story trophy is unmissable — all 12 unlock automatically as you play the story, so there is nothing to protect there. The list below is built from the official descriptions and the published guides, and it says plainly where a trigger is not pinned down yet.

Why the rest are safe

Every other trophy is earned in free roam, in repeatable activities, or by finishing story content that stays available after the credits. The 100% completion checklist opens after the story and earlier progress counts toward it, so nothing on the road to A Long Road can be missed either (GTABase).

Sources & method

Official names, descriptions and grades come from the official list as Exophase and PSNProfiles publish it — the two agree on every name, description and grade. Counts (such as 250 stunts or 42 Shooting Range challenges) come from PSNProfiles' tracker. Roadmap timing, the difficulty rating and the one missable flag come from PowerPyx's GTA 6 trophy guide, cross-checked against GTABase's trophy list. Every guide on this page is written in our own words for this tracker; where a trigger is not pinned down in any published guide yet, the guide says so instead of inventing a location.

    How this tracker works

    Your ticks and counters are saved in this browser only (localStorage) — nothing leaves your machine. Export downloads them as a JSON file; import reads one back. The spoiler setting hides the names, descriptions and guides of the 28 hidden trophies until you earn them. This page loads no network resources at all and works with the network off.
